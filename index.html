Service Line

Follow the line — every module is a station on this network

  1. Stage 01

    Infrared surveillance

    Two opposing cameras monitor the tank from both sides.

  2. Stage 02

    Fire detected

    The front-facing infrared sensor identifies the flame at TK-001.

  3. Stage 03

    Alarm signal to FACP-01

    The camera signal travels along the cable to the fire alarm control panel.

  4. Stage 04

    V-102 actuates

    The panel commands the deluge sequence; the valve beside the panel opens first.

  5. Stage 05

    V-101 opens

    The second deluge valve opens to supply the tank protection system.

  6. Stage 06

    Water and foam ready

    The overhead sprinkler and low-level foam systems are enabled at TK-001.
